Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Aurantia collects when you visit our website or place an order, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Order details you give us: your name, email address, WhatsApp number, delivery address, city and any order note.
  • What you order: the products, quantities, prices and payment method.
  • Payment status: whether a payment succeeded and its reference number. We never see or store your card or wallet details; these are entered on Safepay's secure page.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, WhatsApp or Instagram.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser type when pages load.

Why we use it

  • To process, pack and deliver your order.
  • To send your order confirmation by email and WhatsApp, and to contact you about delivery or payment.
  • To answer your questions and handle exchanges or complaints.
  • To keep records required for accounting, tax and fraud prevention.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us run the shop, and only what they need:

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, exchanges and our accounting and legal obligations, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner where we are not required to keep it.

Your choices

  • Ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Tell us to stop contacting you on WhatsApp (reply STOP or message us).

Message us using the details below and we will reply within 7 days.

Children

Our shop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Customers under 18 should order with a parent or guardian's permission.

Keeping it safe

The site uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, payments are handled by Safepay, and order records are only accessible to the Aurantia team. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
