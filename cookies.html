Cookie Policy

This page explains the small amount of information our website saves in your browser, and why.

The short version

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics. The only thing our own site saves is your shopping bag, so it's still there if you close the tab.

What is saved

NameSet byPurposeHow long
aurantia-bagAurantiaRemembers the items in your bagUntil you place an order or clear your browser data
aurantia-noticeAurantiaRemembers that you've seen the storage noticeUntil you clear your browser data

These are stored using your browser's "local storage" rather than cookies, and they never leave your device. They are needed for the bag to work.

Other services

  • Safepay: when you choose to pay online, you go to Safepay's own page, which may set cookies needed for secure payment and fraud prevention. Those are covered by Safepay's own policy.
  • Google Fonts: our fonts load from Google, which receives your IP address and browser type to deliver them.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram: if you tap a WhatsApp or Instagram link, those apps apply their own policies.

Your choices

You can clear or block site storage in your browser settings at any time. The site will still work, but your bag won't be remembered between visits.
